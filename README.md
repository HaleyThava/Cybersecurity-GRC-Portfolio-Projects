Cybersecurity governance, risk, and compliance work focused on the Australian healthcare sector. This repository documents portfolio projects by applying recognised GRC frameworks to realistic healthcare scenarios. This project will assess maturity, identify risks, and produce practical recommendations grounded in Australian regulatory context.

### Cyber Pharmacy — Cybersecurity Maturity and Risk Assessment

A combined NIST CSF 2.0 maturity assessment and NIST SP 800-30 risk assessment for Cyber Pharmacy, conducted under Australian healthcare regulatory context including the Privacy Act 1988, Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, My Health Records Act 2012, and Medicines and Poisons Act 2014 (WA).

**Frameworks and standards applied:**
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 — maturity assessment across 20 subcategories spanning all six Functions
- NIST SP 800-30 Revision 1 — qualitative risk assessment methodology and risk matrix
- ASD Essential Eight — referenced for control alignment
- Australian Privacy Principles, NDB scheme, MHR Act 2012, Medicines and Poisons Act 2014 (WA) — regulatory context

**The report includes:**
- Business context and threat landscape for a community pharmacy environment
- Maturity scoring across Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover Functions
- Risk register of 8 prioritised risks with likelihood/impact ratings
- Risk-to-control mapping linking risks to NIST CSF 2.0 subcategories
- Prioritised recommendations grouped by horizon (high priority within 6 months, medium priority within 12 months)
- 12-month implementation roadmap phased across 3, 6, and 12-month milestones
- Full subcategory-level maturity assessment register (Appendix A)

**Healthcare lens:** The assessment treats cybersecurity as a patient safety, privacy and business continuity issue — not solely an IT issue. Findings reference real world pharmacy operational details including Schedule 8 controlled drug compliance, QCPP quality framework parallels, AHPRA and Pharmacy Board of Australia obligations, MHR session integrity and the NDB 30-day assessment window.

## What this portfolio demonstrates

- Application of internationally recognised frameworks (NIST CSF 2.0, NIST SP 800-30) to a real-world healthcare scenario
- Mapping of cybersecurity controls to Australian regulatory obligations
- Risk based prioritisation of recommendations appropriate to organisational scale and resources
- Translation of technical findings into business language for non-technical decision-makers
- Awareness of healthcare specific operational, regulatory, and clinical safety considerations
